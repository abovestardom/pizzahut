Pizza Hut Buffet

The all-you-can-eat legend

Remember the Pizza Hut buffet? The red cups, the salad bar, slice after slice of hot, fresh pizza sliding out of the kitchen — the all-you-can-eat legend of the '80s and '90s never really left our hearts. For a generation, the Pizza Hut buffet wasn't just lunch; it was a rite of passage, a reward, a Friday-night tradition.

Now the Pizza Hut buffet is making a comeback at locations across the country, and a whole new crowd is discovering what the fuss was about: unlimited pizza, pasta, salad, and breadsticks, all dine-in, all you can eat.

pizzahutbuffet.com is home base for buffet believers. We're throwing a party to celebrate it properly — and this page is the lore archive: the story, the spread, and answers to every buffet question.

Buffet questions

Does Pizza Hut still have a buffet?

Yes! Select Pizza Hut locations have brought back the all-you-can-eat buffet — unlimited pizza, pasta, salad, and breadsticks, dine-in only. Availability varies by location, so call ahead before you go.

What food is on the Pizza Hut buffet?

The classic Pizza Hut buffet spreads out unlimited pizza (pepperoni, cheese, supreme, and rotating specials), pasta, a full salad bar, breadsticks, and sometimes dessert options like cinnamon rolls — all you can eat, dine-in only.
